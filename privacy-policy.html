Privacy Policy for Accessibility Text Enhancer

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Thank you for using the Accessibility Text Enhancer extension ("the Extension"). This privacy policy explains what information the Extension handles and how it is used.

Data Collection

The Accessibility Text Enhancer is committed to protecting your privacy. We handle only the minimum information necessary to provide core functionality.

1. User Preferences (Chrome Storage)

The Extension uses Chrome's storage.sync API to remember your dark/light toolbar theme, your reading preferences (voice, speed, pitch, ruler source, and focus dim), your text styling defaults (font, spacing, highlight colour, and highlight style), your translation target language, and your per-site on/off rules. Reading presets write the same text styling defaults. Chrome may sync this data across the devices signed in to your Google account, under Google's terms; it is never transmitted to the developer or any third party. The extension's global on/off switch uses storage.local, so it stays on the device where you set it.

2. Website Access and User Interactions

To enhance pages, the Extension runs on the websites you visit and reads the text you select and the styles of the element containing it. It also responds to text selection, toolbar clicks, and keyboard shortcuts so it can show the toolbar and apply the enhancement you request. This processing happens in your browser. Page content and interaction data are never sent to the developer or any third party, and the Extension does not run on browser-internal pages such as chrome:// or the Chrome Web Store.

3. No Collection or Transmission of Personal Information

The Extension does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Browsing history
  • Passwords or form data
  • Financial information
  • Cookies or tracking data
  • Page content beyond the transient local processing described in Section 2
  • Logs of clicks, keystrokes, mouse movements, scrolling, or other interaction data

4. Translation Processing

The "Translate" feature uses Chrome's built-in Translator API. The selected text and the translation result are processed in your browser. The translated text replaces the selected text in the page for the session, and the original is kept in memory until Undo, Reset page, or the page unloads. The first time a language pair is used, Chrome downloads an on-device language pack; the Extension does not send page text to the developer or any third party, and it does not store the selected text or the translation result beyond the page session. The target language is a preference stored as described in Section 1.

5. Text-to-Speech Processing

The "Read Aloud" feature uses your browser's built-in Web Speech API. The Extension does not send page text to its own servers. Depending on the voices installed on your system, your browser or operating system may synthesize speech locally or through the voice provider's service; the Extension does not receive or store that data.

6. No Performance Metrics

The Extension does not collect or transmit performance metrics. Any measurements reported in the project's research were gathered separately during testing, not through the Extension.

Limited Use

The Extension's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to any third party. We do not use analytics services or tracking pixels.

Third-Party Links

The Extension operates on various websites but has no control over the content or privacy practices of those external sites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We encourage users to frequently check this page for any changes.

Consent

By using the Extension, you consent to this privacy policy.
